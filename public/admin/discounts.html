Discounts

Give one-off discounts to individual delivery / online platform orders, and set up automatic rules that apply platform-wide.

Automatic Discount Rules

Set discounts that apply automatically to online platform orders — for the whole platform, a specific food item, a specific time of day, and/or specific days of the week. These affect every matching order automatically; they are not applied per transaction. If more than one rule matches an order, only the single best (highest-value) discount is applied.
